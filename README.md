# GoPro Local Cloud

A free, local, open-source alternative to GoPro Cloud Subscription. Auto-backup your GoPro Hero 9/10/11/12/13 footage to Raspberry Pi, NAS, or PC via USB/WiFi. Free.

Connect your camera via USB to your Raspberry Pi (or PC) and the system will **automatically detect, download, and organize** your files onto your external hard drive.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🚀 **Zero Camera Config:** Just plug in the USB cable and you're good to go.
* 🔄 **Smart Backup:** Detects which files are new. If you already backed something up, it skips it.
* 🛡️ **Fail-Safe:** Did the cable disconnect halfway through a 10GB video? No problem! Next time it will resume the download exactly where it left off.
* 🖥️ **Modern Web UI:** Control everything and view the status from your phone or laptop on the local network.
* 📂 **Multi-Drive Support:** Easily choose which hard drive or USB stick you want to save your videos on.
* 🐳 **Dockerized (Linux/Pi):** Clean, isolated installation that won't mess up your OS.
